    </div>
    <p>
      Human-Centered AI at USC IMPACT LAB focuses on the full path from model development to
      practical use: how systems are framed, how data is represented, how recommendations are
      communicated, and how people ultimately decide whether to trust, question, or override
      an algorithmic output.

  <div>
    <div class="section-heading">
      <p class="eyebrow">Research questions</p>
      <h2 id="questions-title">Questions we ask</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="detail-grid">
      <article>
        <h3>How should AI communicate uncertainty?</h3>
        <p>
          We investigate interfaces and explanations that help people calibrate confidence,
          identify limitations, and make informed decisions under ambiguity.
        </p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>How do intelligent tools fit into real workflows?</h3>
        <p>
          We study how AI systems interact with organizational routines, professional
          expertise, incentives, and constraints in deployed environments.
        </p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>What makes AI accountable?</h3>
        <p>
          We develop evaluation approaches that consider accuracy, fairness, robustness,
          usability, and downstream effects on people and institutions.
        </p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
